Sparkpaw direct renderer-call functional gate
=============================================

This unnumbered 68030 candidate retains the separate Level-1 and Stormrail
renderer objects. The three per-frame renderer operations now branch on the
active section at their call site and use a direct function call; indirect
function pointers have been removed. Gameplay, visuals, assets and state are
unchanged.

Run a short complete functional smoke from the ready menu:

- play Level 1 long enough to check movement, combat, HUD and rendering;
- finish Level 1 and use Continue;
- confirm Stormrail departure and flight render and control normally.

No cadence log is produced in this 68030 gate. Stop after Stormrail is visibly
running and report any corruption, crash, transition problem or changed feel.
