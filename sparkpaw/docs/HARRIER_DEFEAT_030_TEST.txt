SPARKPAW HARRIER DEFEAT - UNNUMBERED 68030 HD VISUAL TEST

Build: Harrier-Test in drawer Harrier-Death-030-HD
This historical focused test preceded the 0.7.0-alpha.13 release.

Use PAL A1200/AGA, 68030, 2 MB Chip and 8 MB Fast in FS-UAE.
Mount this entire drawer as the HD game directory and run Harrier-Test.
Do not mix its assets with a numbered release drawer.

At READY, choose OPTIONS -> START SECTION: STORMRAIL, then start.
This corrected candidate keeps the expanded blast and uses a much denser,
longer three-hit defeat SFX. It begins just before the Harrier encounter.
Fly with the joystick or arrow keys; Fire shoots. Defeat the boss and watch the hull
ruptures, full fireburst, fragments, closed gate, then gate opening and
automatic flight into Stormrail results. Listen for boom, boom, BOOM over
Iron Horizon. Play time should be about one to two minutes.

Please compare the three booms with Sparkpaw's normal shot in both SFX ONLY
and MUSIC + SFX. The first boom should already sound forceful, the final BOOM
should dominate and ring out, without crackling. Also report
visual quality, smoothness, sound balance, any damage or shots after the kill,
wall/HUD corruption, and whether results appears. If easy,
pause with P during combat, resume, and verify controls; Esc returns to READY.
The explosion should play once for each actual victory. A fresh Stormrail
start may replay it after a new kill.

This is a logger-free visual candidate. Left mouse does not save a log and
there is no expected frozen diagnostic screen. Please send a screenshot or
short recording if any visual glitch appears. The minimum-68020 cadence gate
comes only after this 68030 visual/function review.
