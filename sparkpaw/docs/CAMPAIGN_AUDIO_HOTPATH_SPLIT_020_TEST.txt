Sparkpaw audio-hotpath-split Level-1 cadence gate
================================================

This stock-68020 minimal-cadence build uses the 68030-accepted audio split.
Level 1 runs the original common audio update; Stormrail additionally advances
its four Harrier charge/fire cooldowns in audioUpdateStormrail().

Play a representative Level-1 route including busy combat, shots, jumps and
collectibles. After the run, press the left mouse button once. The image
freezes while renderdiag.log is written. Wait until disk writing has stopped,
then stop/reset FS-UAE.

Compare against
---------------

- collision-cache baseline: 49.30 FPS, 1.41% two-field, zero three-plus;
- alpha.68: 48.58 FPS, 2.9% two-field, zero three-plus;
- direct-call predecessor: 48.42 FPS, 3.26% two-field.

This is a marginal candidate. Retain it as a performance improvement only if
the normalized distribution is reproducibly no worse; audio ownership remains
a valid architectural separation independently of cadence noise.
