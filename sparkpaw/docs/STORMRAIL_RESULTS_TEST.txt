Stormrail results user test
===========================

Machine: FS-UAE A1200/68030 HD. Launch `Sparkpaw-Results` from this drawer.
This is an unnumbered focused candidate, not alpha.68 and not a release.

Play the complete resident interlude from the short departure cliff. Board by
jumping into the visible Skimmer cockpit, finish the full route, destroy the
120-HP Harrier and let the Skimmer pass automatically through the opened gate.

Check that results appear only after that complete passage. The screen must be
the familiar Level-1 screen with exactly `ENEMIES x20`, `DIAMONDS x5`,
`TIME x10`, `SCORE` and `REPLAY LEVEL`. Verify the normal row order, tally tick,
four-field rhythm, faster diamond/time steps, Fire row-skip, release debounce
and final prompt. The time bonus uses a 150-second complete-interlude par.

After the prompt appears, release Fire and press it once. The display must fade
fully to black and return directly to a fresh departure/boarding run. There
must be no Level 1, LOADING/CHARGING screen, disk icon or Workbench transition.
Confirm x3 lives, full health, zero score/time/diamonds, fresh enemies/debris/
pickups and a fresh 120-HP Harrier on the replayed run. In particular, no old
shot, reward, boss damage, score or pickup may survive or award itself again.

Please report the visible tally, timing, tally audio, Fire skip/debounce,
complete black fade, replay transition and overall feel. Reset or stop FS-UAE
when finished; this production-style candidate writes no diagnostic log.
