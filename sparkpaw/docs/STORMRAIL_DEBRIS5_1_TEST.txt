SPARKPAW STORMRAIL DEBRIS 5.1 - PICKUP AND MICRO-VARIATION
===========================================================

Debris 5 is preserved as the accepted-enough fallback baseline. This 68030
candidate changes only two bounded details above it.

Restart contract
----------------

Like Level 1, losing a life restarts the section but does not respawn diamonds
already collected during that level attempt. Their count and score remain too.
This prevents deliberate life-loss farming and is unchanged in this candidate.

Rock loot correction
--------------------

The two large loot rocks previously reused pickup IDs 12 and 13 from the fourth
free diamond line. Collecting that line therefore suppressed their later drop.
The rocks now own unique IDs 32 and 33 through a second 32-bit progress mask.
Destroying each authored large loot rock can reveal one diamond. Once collected,
that diamond remains consumed after life restart, matching every other pickup.

Variation polish
----------------

The accepted Debris-5 routes, density, art, cursor and endpoint are unchanged.
Objects now use five deterministic offscreen start-X positions instead of one,
and tumble phase is derived from unique event ID rather than reused pool slot.
This removes a small mechanical cadence without introducing runtime randomness.

Please verify that at least one large destructible rock visibly drops a diamond,
and whether the entrances feel slightly less patterned. Press LMB only when you
want to freeze/save the diagnostic state.
