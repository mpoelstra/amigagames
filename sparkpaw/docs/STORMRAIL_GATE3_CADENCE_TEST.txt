Stormrail Gate 3 cadence test - FS-UAE/68020
============================================

This compile-guarded direct-start candidate contains the user-accepted Gate-3
enemy, formation, projectile, collision, sound, score and diamond workload.
Only the low-overhead cadence and renderer-ownership counters are enabled. It
does not change alpha.68, campaign progression, Level 2 or ADF packaging.

Run
---

1. Use the stock FS-UAE/68020 configuration.
2. Start StormG3-Cadence and board the Skimmer normally.
3. Fly and fight normally through all four formations. Move in every direction,
   collect diamonds, dodge Orb fire and use held autofire for part of the run.
4. The diagnostic ignores the arbitrary boarding time, measures exactly 1,000
   Flight intervals from flight tick 400 through 1,400, then automatically
   freezes while writing renderdiag.log; no mouse click is needed.
5. Wait three seconds, then stop FS-UAE. It will not return to Workbench.
6. Leave renderdiag.log in this drawer for Codex to inspect.

Please report whether scrolling, movement, enemies, shots, pickups and audio
looked and felt smooth. The comparison baseline is the accepted empty-flight
Gate 2/2.5 run: 49.26 FPS over 1,000 intervals, 992 one-field intervals, zero
ownership violations and 214/214 requested/started shots.
