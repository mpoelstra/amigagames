Sparkpaw Stage 5H2: corrected sprite-safe AGA64 origin proof
============================================================

Stage 5H measured raw FMODE3 exactly 16 logical pixels to the right of the
accepted FMODE1 reference. This isolated proof applies only that measured
`+16` phase correction while retaining:

- sprite-safe DDF `$30..$d0`;
- FMODE3 64-bit bitplane fetch;
- eight-byte bitmap alignment and a 48-byte fetch;
- the complete 0..63 fine-delay range and 64-pixel coarse pointer.

Test in FS-UAE/68030 HD. Capture a screenshot during the initial three-second
phase-zero hold. Then allow at least eight seconds of sweep and watch the
63-to-0 wrap for a jump, corrupt edge or moving lower reference. Click left
mouse to write `renderbench.log`. A movie is needed only if a temporal defect
is visible.

The marker must occupy the same position as the Stage 5H FMODE1 reference
(capture x=156..171 at the unchanged host window size). This proof does not
contain the production Sparkpaw sprite and therefore cannot itself accept
sprite DMA, gameplay, performance, 68020, ADF or real hardware.
