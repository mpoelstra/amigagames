Sparkpaw early two-Strider scene trace
======================================

This is byte-identical to the accepted 49.30-FPS collision-cache executable.
It changes no gameplay, renderer, audio, assets or instrumentation. This run is
shortened only to retain the early busy scene inside the existing 1,024-frame
trace ring.

Test procedure
--------------

1. Start Level 1 normally.
2. Play through the busy early encounter containing two Striders and the other
   nearby enemies.
3. Immediately after passing or clearing that encounter, press the left mouse
   button once. Do not finish the level first.
4. The image freezes while renderdiag.log is written. Wait several seconds
   until writing has stopped, then stop/reset FS-UAE.

The goal is diagnosis, not another subjective whole-level comparison. The log
must retain the exact scene's update, Bob, camera and object-count history.
