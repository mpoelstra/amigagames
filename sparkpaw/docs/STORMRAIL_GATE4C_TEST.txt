SPARKPAW STORMRAIL DEBRIS 1.0 - 68030 HD REFERENCE
===================================================

This is the preserved Debris 1.0 gameplay reference. It preserves the accepted four
enemy formations and replaces the sparse Gate-4B passage with a deterministic
28-event, six-slot ruin field inspired by the supplied Zynaps recording.

Run the drawer's Sparkpaw executable with the usual A1200/68030 FS-UAE setup.
After the fourth enemy wave, judge the following:

1. The passage should feel continuously busy and somewhat claustrophobic, with
   moving gaps that require steering rather than an impossible solid wall.
2. Arches, slabs, pillars and fragments move at visibly different speeds and
   gently drift vertically while their prebuilt frames suggest tumbling.
3. Solid debris consumes shots and plays an impact sound but does not flash.
   Destructible debris flashes after a hit, so the rule remains learnable.
4. A large breakable arch takes six hits, becomes visibly cracked for its last
   three hit points and releases a normal Sparkpaw diamond when destroyed.
5. Debris contact costs one half-heart and plays the accepted hurt sound.
6. All four earlier formations, enemy fire, scoring, restart behaviour,
   pickups, parallax and Skimmer controls must remain unchanged.

Please report whether the field is exciting or merely cluttered, whether the
tumble reads at native scale, whether the large loot object is findable in the
pressure, and any mask trail, loading hang, stale object or slowdown.

This is a 68030 feel gate only. No 1UP, clouds, route extension, finale, Level 2
or campaign integration is present, and no 68020 performance verdict is being
claimed yet.
