This is the post-visual-acceptance performance gate for the complete stationary
Harrier encounter. It starts immediately before distance 15500 and measures
combat only. Setup, gate opening, automatic exit and idle time after the fight
are excluded. Gameplay, art, dust, 120 HP, half-health Hunter phase, sounds and
the existing 5/4 projectile pools are identical to accepted v12.

display or clean targets is prohibited after the rejected v14/v15 corruption.

proves that its authored non-transparent bounds are exactly 63x45 at local
offset (8,1), so the planar cache and restore/draw rectangle omit transparent
border pixels. The logical 80x46 actor, collision, movement, attacks, art and

45.67 FPS over 1,406 intervals, 127 two-field and 3 three-field intervals.

all 208 rows when nothing damaged them. Each rolling target gets one complete

No gate pixels are cached into clean/display targets. Compare primarily with
v16: 48.02 FPS over 1,192 intervals, 42 two-field and 3 three-plus intervals.
