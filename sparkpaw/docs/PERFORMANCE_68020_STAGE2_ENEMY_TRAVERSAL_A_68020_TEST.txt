Sparkpaw Stage 2 - enemy traversal A: linear search (FS-UAE/68020 HD)

Reference variant. Run Sparkpaw-A-Linear-Search from this drawer with the
normal FS-UAE/68020 configuration.

Test route:
1. Play long enough to pass several Striders and traverse platforms in both
   directions.
2. Run far enough away that earlier Striders are parked, then return toward
   them. Include jumping, shooting and at least one busy enemy section.
3. Check that Strider movement, platform traversal, firing, enemy graphics,
   diamonds, HUD and the ground/HUD seam remain normal.
4. Click the left mouse button once at the end to flush renderdiag.log in this
   drawer, then quit or reset.

Do not rename or move the log. Variant B has its own drawer and log.
