SPARKPAW STORMRAIL DEBRIS 4 - ONE AUTHORED FIELD
================================================

Run only Sparkpaw-Debris4 with the usual A1200/68030 FS-UAE setup.

This replaces the rejected uniform Debris-3 stream. It is one continuous
48-event debris field driven by monotone stormrailDistance, not two joined
sections and not a repeating generator. Event spacing, lanes, speed and drift
are explicitly authored across the complete passage.

The visible mix is deliberately rebalanced to eight large 48x40 ruin blocks,
sixteen 32x40 pillar/masonry pieces and twenty-four 16x16 shards. All three
families and all approved prebuilt tumble pairs are used. Large blocks use only
medium/fast leftward motion and the runtime still permits at most one large
block at once. No object bounces or reverses direction.

Two destructible large blocks are guaranteed a pool slot and can reveal one
diamond each. A reservation may retire only the oldest ordinary small/medium
cue already nearest the left exit; it never exceeds the six-object pool and
cannot create a delayed tail.

The last authored debris event is at distance 11130. Debris must visibly drain
to zero. The temporary slice continues scrolling to distance 15200, leaving
4070 empty distance units (just over twenty seconds at cruise speed) for later
enemy-pattern and finale work. It then reaches one hard test endpoint; it must
not loop or restart the debris.

Please judge:

1. Does variation remain convincing through the final third rather than
   collapsing into identical horizontal or diagonal groups?
2. Are large blocks, pillars and different shards visible throughout?
3. Does the field feel like one sustained Zynaps-like organised chaos passage?
4. Do both large loot blocks read clearly and produce a diamond when destroyed?
5. Does the debris end cleanly, followed by a clearly long empty route reserve?

Press LMB only when you want to freeze/save the diagnostic state.
