Sparkpaw Level-1 and Stormrail audio-update split
================================================

This unnumbered 68030 functional candidate restores the alpha.68 audio-update
path for Level 1. Stormrail calls a small extension that additionally advances
the four Harrier charge/fire cooldowns. Sample ownership, Paula channels,
priorities, cooldown values and audio assets are unchanged.

Run a short functional smoke:

- in Level 1, check shooting, enemy hit/death, jump and collect sounds;
- finish Level 1 and select Continue;
- in Stormrail, check ordinary shot audio and reach enough of the finale to
  hear at least one Harrier charge/fire cue if practical.

This build has no cadence logger. Report missing, stuck, repeated or otherwise
changed audio, plus any gameplay or transition regression.
