Stormrail Gate 3.6 - FS-UAE/68030 HD review
===========================================

This focused unnumbered candidate starts at the accepted short cliff approach.
It does not change alpha.68, campaign progression, Level 2 or ADF packaging.

Controls
--------

Joystick/arrow keys move Sparkpaw and the Skimmer. Jump into the open cockpit.
Fire/Space fires rapid plasma. The interlude then enters open flight.

Review
------

Play through all four enemy formations and free diamond lines (roughly 55 seconds
of flight). Check that:

- five small fast Darts enter as one readable wave and exit cleanly;
- four visually different Pulse Orbs follow a slower crossing curve;
- the third high/low Dart split keeps both lower enemies continuously visible
  above the HUD instead of disappearing and reappearing at the lower boundary;
- Orb fire is sparse, left-moving and avoidable;
- Orb fire uses the same clearly red/orange animated pulse as Level-1 Striders,
  never Sparkpaw's cyan player-shot palette;
- a fresh Fire press shoots immediately; held autofire repeats about 5.6 times
  per second and deliberate tapping is capped around 8.3 times per second;
- shots hit, remove and destroy enemies without residue;
- held autofire cannot destroy enemies wholly outside the right edge; hits
  begin naturally once roughly half of an entering silhouette is visible;
- each Dart defeat adds 20 points, each tougher Pulse Orb adds 40 and every
  diamond adds the existing 5; there is no oversized direct formation bonus;
- ordinary hits, enemy destruction and diamond pickup have distinct existing
  Level-1 sounds;
- enemy contact or an Orb pulse removes half a heart, plays hurt audio and
  grants a short flashing invulnerability window;
- four deterministic free-flight diamond lines appear between combat beats,
  never more than four diamonds at once, and each pickup sounds/updates once;
- after six accepted hits one life is consumed, full health returns and open
  flight restarts without replaying the cliff; collected diamonds stay gone
  and previously defeated formation members cannot award score a second time;
- destroying every member before escape creates four existing Sparkpaw diamonds;
- the pause between patterns leaves room to move, aim and dodge;
- boarding, fade, pilot, controls, rapid fire, bounds, parallax, route palettes
  and fixed HUD still match the accepted Gate-2/2.5 baseline.

Please report the visual/function verdict for this exact 68030 drawer. Do not
run the 68020 cadence gate yet; it will be prepared only after this gate passes.
