Sparkpaw instant Level-1 replay candidate
==========================================

Target: A1200/68030 HD (also compiles and completes the automatic proof on
FS-UAE/68020).

What changed
------------

- The final score prompt now reads REPLAY LEVEL.
- Fire still fades the score screen completely to black.
- Level 1 then restarts from resident gameplay assets and renderer buffers;
  there is no LOADING screen and no DOS asset reload.
- Both rolling playfield targets are restored from the clean canonical world
  before the new gameplay Copper list is published. This is specifically meant
  to prevent the old score/reload corruption from returning.

Please test
-----------

1. Complete Level 1 normally by collecting the Stormstone Core.
2. Let the tally finish, or skip rows with Fire.
3. Confirm that the final prompt says REPLAY LEVEL.
4. Press Fire once.
5. Check that Level 1 returns quickly with no LOADING screen, corrupt frame,
   old Core/1UP/enemy fragments, wrong HUD values, audio glitch or input carry.
6. Walk and fire for a few seconds after the replay to expose delayed damage.

Automatic native proof already passed on FS-UAE A1200/68030 and A1200/68020:
both reached gameplay frame 11 after resident replay at camera x=0, with
1,049,392 bytes Chip RAM free and a 985,984-byte largest Chip block. This is
bounded emulator evidence, not acceptance of the complete manual transition.

The first manual candidate was rejected because staggered diamond updates drew
some newly initialized Bobs temporarily at y=0. This build initializes every
diamond at its authored spawn position before the first resident frame. The
repeated 68030 and 68020 native proofs report `collectibles=valid` after replay.
