SPARKPAW - STORMRAIL STEP 1: CLIFF BOARDING

This unnumbered Phase 6D build tests only the short Level-1-like cliff approach
and physical jump into the hovering Skimmer. It skips the intro and Level 1,
does not change campaign progression and does not change the current ADF.

RUN
  Use FS-UAE with an A1200 / 68030 configuration.
  Mount dist and open: Stormrail-Step1-Cliff-030-HD
  Start: Stormrail-Test

CONTROLS
  Left/right: move Sparkpaw.
  Up or configured jump button: jump.
  Jump from the broken cliff and descend into the open cockpit.
  Walking off or missing the Skimmer drops Sparkpaw and resets the approach.
  Fire, proximity and waiting do not board the Skimmer.

WHAT TO CHECK
  1. The raised Level-1-style ruin reads immediately as a solid cliff.
  2. Its broken right edge makes it obvious that walking is no continuation.
  3. The Skimmer feels close enough for a comfortable deliberate jump.
  4. The waiting Skimmer visibly hovers very slightly and already shows the
     same small cyan glow attached to its rear hull as the occupied craft.
  5. Descending into the cockpit catches Sparkpaw reliably without feeling
     like a teleport or requiring pixel-perfect positioning.
  6. The Skimmer hull keeps exactly the same size throughout boarding while
     Sparkpaw keeps one fixed scale through contact, settle, active sit and a
     a light lean and a final clearly laid-back pose behind the cockpit rim.
  7. Sparkpaw first settles into the cockpit at the exact parked position;
     the craft pauses briefly and only then glides smoothly toward its later
     flight start position while rising.
  8. Missing the jump resets before Sparkpaw disappears behind the HUD.
  9. The forward controls-check pose remains briefly, then Sparkpaw eases back
     through two poses into a clearly laid-back posture before movement.

TEST END
  This gate intentionally stops on the successful boarded pose. Open-flight
  controls, enemies, formations, diamonds, hazards and the finale belong to
  later separately reviewed steps. Press the left mouse button when finished;
  the image freezes while stormrail-proof.log is saved. Wait a few seconds,
  then stop or reset FS-UAE. It does not return to Workbench.
