SPARKPAW STORMRAIL PACING 2 - 68030 FEEL TEST
================================================

This candidate keeps the accepted Debris 2 native art, animation, collision,
hit feedback and six-object pool. All 48 distance events are re-authored as one
continuous passage at the same intended visual density: 16 large blocks, eight
pillar fragments and 24 shards are interleaved throughout. Speeds remain in
the accepted 2--4 pixel range but are balanced so slow large blocks cannot
accumulate after faster small pieces disappear. It is not split into an old
section and a separate add-on.

Earlier encounter pacing is also slightly tighter. Complete-formation rewards
enter sooner, travel faster, use 18-pixel spacing and cycle through four compact
diamond patterns. Free pickup lines were moved so they do not block formation
rewards in the four-slot reward pool.

Run Sparkpaw-Pacing2 with the usual A1200/68030 FS-UAE setup. Please judge:

1. Does the whole debris field retain the accepted organised-chaos feel?
2. Is it similarly crowded throughout, without a sparse or repetitive tail?
3. Do large blocks, pillar fragments and small shards remain visibly varied?
4. Are the earlier waves separated by enough breathing room despite arriving
   somewhat sooner?
5. Do formation-reward diamonds appear promptly and in tighter clear patterns?
6. Report any missing object, mask trail, loading hang, collision oddity or
   slowdown.

After the final debris objects leave, this development slice intentionally
continues as empty open flight. That is not additional level content and no
more debris should appear; the later enemy section and finale are not built.

This is a 68030 visual/feel gate. It does not yet add ruin-dust particles,
post-debris enemy patterns, hazards, the finale, Level 2 or campaign integration.
The accepted Debris 2 cadence build remains archived as the rollback baseline.
