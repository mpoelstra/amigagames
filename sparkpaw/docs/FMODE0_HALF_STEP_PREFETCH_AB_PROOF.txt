Sparkpaw Stage 5K: FMODE0 half-step prefetch A/B proof
=====================================================

This production rolling-ring proof tests the narrowest remaining form of Stage
5G's successful left-edge geometry while retaining Sparkpaw's current three
attached 16-pixel pairs. Both builds use FMODE0, identical gameplay, the real
512x3 FRONT ring, alternating targets, REAR parallax and the fixed HUD split.

- DDF30-Reference uses the accepted `$30..$d0` 42-byte gameplay fetch.
- DDF28-Candidate begins exactly one 16-pixel word earlier at `$28`, fetches
  44 bytes and supplies real two-byte guards for FRONT and REAR. The HUD
  explicitly returns to its accepted `$30..$d0` 42-byte fetch.

FS-UAE/68030 HD procedure
-------------------------

Run the reference first. Move right and back left across several camera
boundaries, jump, fire and reach enemies; click left mouse to flush its log.
Reset FS-UAE, repeat with the candidate and flush its separate log.

For each build report independently:

- whether every colour and component of Sparkpaw remains visible;
- black/displaced left strips or apparent world jumps;
- pixels moving at the left ground/HUD boundary;
- enemy, projectile, parallax or collision regressions.

The candidate passes this first gate only if its entire presentation matches
the reference. Performance is recorded but is not the purpose of this proof.
Do not test FS-UAE/68020 or real hardware yet. This is not an ADF, release or
main-distribution candidate.

First supplied run: invalid candidate build
-------------------------------------------

The DDF30 reference was reported visually and functionally correct. After
Charging, the DDF28 candidate showed a completely black display while gameplay
input and firing audio continued; left mouse also flushed a 747-frame,
50.00-FPS log with successful publications and zero ownership violations.

This is not a hardware result. Source inspection found that the conditional
candidate patch overwrote the Copper MOVE register-address word with `$28`
instead of emitting DDFSTRT `$92,$28`. The malformed list explains the live
simulation behind a black display. Correct and structurally verify the Copper
pair before repeating this gate; do not mark the `$28` technique rejected.
