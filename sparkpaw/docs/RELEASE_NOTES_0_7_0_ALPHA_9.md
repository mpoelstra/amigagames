# Sparkpaw 0.7.0-alpha.9 — Drowned Turbines

## What's new since alpha.8

- Continue from Stormrail Skimmer into **Drowned Turbines**, a third playable section with flooded platforms, moving pontoons, water jets, waterfalls and a final Rain Core encounter.
- Face new turbine crabs, pump walkers and Spillwings. Find the checkpoint, collect diamonds and health, then review your Drowned results or replay the section.
- Carry lives, health, diamonds and score across all three sections. OPTIONS can start directly at Drowned Turbines.
- Hear **Undertow Circuit** in Drowned Turbines. The HD and WHDLoad SOUNDTEST also include its Pump Shot and Checkpoint effects. The approved Drowned music is unchanged from the tested campaign candidates.
- The floppy edition now spans three disks. It retains the familiar title start and shows a matching **INSERT DISK 3** message when needed; it has no story intro or SOUNDTEST. Requested disks can be found in DF0, DF1, DF2 or DF3.
- Second-button detection and keyboard input handling have been adjusted. A regression playtest passed on an unspecified configuration. Reports from affected users have not yet confirmed whether their original input problem is resolved. An OPTIONS trigger and a possible 68060 link remain unproven.

Requires a PAL A1200/AGA, 68020 or better, 2 MB Chip RAM and 8 MB Fast RAM. The HD, WHDLoad and three-disk ADF campaign candidates were played and approved in FS-UAE before the input corrections. Short alpha.9 HD, WHDLoad and ADF checks also worked in FS-UAE, including Disk 3 in DF2/DF3. Real A1200 and physical-floppy behavior of this alpha remain unverified.
