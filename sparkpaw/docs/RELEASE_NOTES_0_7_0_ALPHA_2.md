# Sparkpaw 0.7.0-alpha.2 — Into Stormrail

## What's new since alpha.68

- Continue from Storm Ruins into the Stormrail Skimmer adventure, carrying
  your remaining lives, health and diamond meter.
- Board the Skimmer and fly through enemy formations, ruined debris and health
  pickups toward the Harrier finale and a new results screen.
- Replay either section, return to the title or choose your starting section
  through the ready-screen options.
- New two-disk ADF edition with styled INSERT DISK prompts and automatic use
  of Disk 2 in DF1. The story introduction remains in HD and WHDLoad.
- Updated HD and WHDLoad packages contain the full campaign.

PAL A1200/AGA, 68020+, 2 MB Chip and 8 MB Fast. HD and the two-ADF edition have
user approval in FS-UAE. The new campaign WHDLoad build and physical hardware/
Analogue Pocket compatibility still need testing. Feedback is welcome.
