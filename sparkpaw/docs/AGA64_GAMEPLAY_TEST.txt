Sparkpaw Stage 5I: sprite-safe AGA64 gameplay candidate
=======================================================

This diagnostic HD build transfers the Stage 5H2-accepted corrected FMODE3
geometry into gameplay. Scrolling playfields use FMODE3, DDF `$30..$d0`, a
48-byte fetch, eight-byte aligned pointers, 64-pixel coarse steps and the
measured logical `+16` phase. The HUD explicitly returns to its accepted
FMODE1/44-byte fetch. Sparkpaw remains the unchanged 48x48, 15-colour actor
using three attached 16-pixel hardware-sprite pairs.

First gate: FS-UAE/68030 HD
---------------------------

Run in both directions through multiple 64-pixel camera boundaries. Exercise
sprint, jump, firing, collectibles, beetles and the first Striders. Check:

- Sparkpaw retains every colour and body segment in every animation;
- no black or displaced strip appears at the left gameplay edge;
- HUD, parallax, world alignment and collision remain correct;
- no teleport, ghost trails, corrupt Bobs or wrap hitch appears.

Click left mouse after the run to flush `renderdiag.log`. Provide screenshots
for any static defect and a movie only for a temporal defect. Do not test
FS-UAE/68020 or real hardware until this presentation gate is reviewed. This
is not an ADF or release candidate.

Supplied result: rejected
-------------------------

The supplied FS-UAE/68030 HD movie shows that Sparkpaw's hardware-sprite
components are restored, but the scrolling playfield origin jumps in discrete
steps and variable-width black strips reappear at the left edge. Small pixels
also flicker at the left ground/HUD boundary. Sparkpaw and world objects stay
coherent with one another while the world shifts relative to the fixed HUD;
this is a display-origin regression, not evidence of gameplay-state teleport.

The clean-exit log still reports 49.87 effective FPS over 1,172 frames, three
ring wraps and zero target-ownership violations. Those metrics prove that the
candidate is fast and respects its software buffer-ownership contract; they do
not prove that the Copper displays the correct 64-pixel fetch phase.

Stage 5H2 therefore remains accepted only as an isolated linear-bitmap origin
measurement. Its coverage omitted the production rolling ring, alternating
targets, dual playfield, hardware sprites and FMODE3-to-FMODE1 HUD transition
acting together. Stage 5I is rejected and must not enter the main distribution.
