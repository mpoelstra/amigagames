SPARKPAW STORMRAIL SUNLIT DUST V5 - FS-UAE 68030 HD

This focused build starts at the accepted Stormrail interlude and keeps its
complete debris and enemy-pattern baseline. Gate 4D adds eight independent
blue dust/grit slots. Each travels continuously from right to left with its
own height, phase and speed. Seven accepted vertical positions are unchanged;
only the redundant eighth position moves into the previously empty top strip.
Slow cached white/light-blue/amber changes suggest changing sunlight;
gameplay, pools and collision are unchanged.

Please play the complete route once on the 68030 configuration and judge:

- do the marks read as independent dust/grit rather than clusters;
- is the field sufficiently present while remaining behind gameplay;
- do the slow colour changes feel like light rather than blinking;
- is motion smooth during the busiest debris and five-enemy moments.

Do not save a cadence log for this visual gate. Report what you saw and felt.
