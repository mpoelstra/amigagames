SPARKPAW STORMRAIL DEBRIS 5.2 - SLOW LOOT ROCK
================================================

Debris 5 remains the accepted-enough fallback baseline. Debris 5.2 retains the
5.1 unique rock-drop IDs and micro-variation. The second large loot rock travels
left at three pixels per tick instead of four; all other large rocks remain
fast. Medium and small debris no longer settles into horizontal passes through
the middle: only three horizontal accents remain in upper/lower lanes, while
the other pieces follow shallow straight diagonals.

This gives one clearly longer aiming window for the six hits needed to crack
and destroy it. Its authored 600-distance gap to the following large rock is
greater than its complete 512-distance crossing lifetime, so two large rocks
still cannot overlap. Destroying it must reveal its unique diamond (ID 33).

Please check whether this one slower loot target is easy to recognize, remains
challenging to finish, and visibly produces a diamond. Press LMB only when you
want to freeze/save the diagnostic state.

On life loss the formations respawn, but diamonds already collected from their
rewards remain consumed. Only uncollected reward diamonds can appear again.
