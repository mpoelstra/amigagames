Sparkpaw - Stormrail music (unnumbered main HD candidate)

Launch Sparkpaw-Music. First gate: PAL A1200 / 68030, 2 MB Chip + 8 MB Fast.
Skip the story intro if desired. At READY choose OPTIONS, select STORMRAIL,
then START GAME. No need to replay Level 1 first.

Iron Horizon should play from departure through flight and the Harrier fight.
Listen for the melody, energy and balance with shots, impacts, debris, hurt,
pickups and the Harrier charge/fire cues. No extra load at the finale.
Life loss keeps the music running. Results use the original tally sounds;
REPLAY LEVEL restarts the music from the beginning. Escape returns to READY
with Neon Sky. Level 1 still uses Copper Sprint.

This is ordinary gameplay: no automatic input, diagnostics or LMB log save.
Report how the track feels and any missing/unclear effect or transition fault.
Stop/reset the emulator normally. Existing release files remain unchanged.
