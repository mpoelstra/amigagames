Sparkpaw Stage 5L - isolated 64-pixel AGA player-sprite proof
================================================================

Purpose
-------
This candidate keeps the accepted Stage 5G playfield geometry and changes only
Sparkpaw's hardware-DMA representation. The unchanged 48x48, 15-colour artwork
is packed into one attached 64-pixel AGA sprite pair on channels 0/1. Pixels
48..63 are transparent padding. No pose, palette, baseline, animation timing,
HUD, collision, physics, enemy or gameplay contract is intentionally changed.

Why this proof exists
---------------------
The early $20 playfield fetch can take DMA slots from later 16-pixel sprite
channels. Stage 5G therefore retained good playfield speed and geometry but
lost parts of the six-channel player. This proof asks AGA to fetch the same
four player planes through the earliest attached pair instead of six channels.
The wide stream uses the AGA 64-bit control/data layout and explicitly aligned
Chip RAM staging buffers.

Test first in FS-UAE 68030 HD
-----------------------------
1. Run Sparkpaw-Wide-Sprite-Proof from this drawer.
2. Let the level load; walk and run both directions.
3. Exercise idle, turn, jump, crouch, shoot, air-fire, hurt and ledge poses.
4. Check that Sparkpaw is complete in both orientations, especially the white
   right-facing half and the left-facing tail seen in Stage 5K2.
5. Also check the left playfield edge, the ground/HUD seam, enemies, shots and
   world position for strips, flicker, trails or jumps.
6. Click the left mouse button once after representative play to write
   renderdiag.log, then quit normally.

Status
------
Host build/tests only. No FS-UAE, ADF, Analogue Pocket or real-hardware
acceptance is claimed until the corresponding user test evidence exists.
