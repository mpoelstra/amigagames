Sparkpaw Stage 5F: AGA32 left prefetch guard proof
==================================================

This is an isolated display proof, not the game. Stage 5E retained exact
FMODE0-relative origin and 50 Hz cadence but exposed phase-dependent black
stripes at the left gameplay edge. WinUAE's FMODE model confirms that
FMODE1/lores uses a 16-CCK, 32-pixel indivisible fetch unit. This proof adds
exactly one complete unit before the visible data:

- DDF changes from `$30..$d0`/44 bytes to `$20..$d0`/48 bytes;
- a 4-byte physical guard precedes logical source x=0;
- pointers remain longword aligned;
- the accepted `camera + 16` extended-delay relation is unchanged;
- the fixed lower reference switches back to `$30..$d0` and 44 bytes.

Test in FS-UAE/68030 HD only. During the first three seconds, confirm that the
cyan/white marker has the same position as the accepted D4 FMODE0 and D5
screenshots. Then let the sweep run at least eight seconds and watch the left
edge continuously: it must remain filled at every phase, with no jump, width
change or lower-reference movement. Click left mouse to write `renderbench.log`.
Supply a movie if any temporal left-edge defect remains; otherwise one initial
screenshot and the log are sufficient.

This proof has no gameplay, performance, 68020, ADF, Analogue Pocket or real-
hardware acceptance.
