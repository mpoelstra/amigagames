# Pre-production direction

## Identity

Sparkpaw is an original amber-orange feline adventurer and young inventor. His navy scarf and teal energy gauntlet create a readable silhouette and a stable shared palette.

## First world: Storm Ruins

- Far parallax: violet storm clouds and mountains
- Mid parallax: pine forest, waterfalls and broken energy towers
- Gameplay layer: mossy stone-and-machine ruins with cyan conduits
- 16x16 foreground tiles
- 32x40 player cells

## Initial moveset

- Run
- Variable jump
- Crouch
- Forward energy shot
- Charged shot
- Hurt, death and victory states

## Original prototype sound set

Jump, landing, energy shot, charged shot, enemy hit, player hurt, collectible, checkpoint, Stormstone and menu selection.

## Boundaries

The project may take broad inspiration from the responsiveness, colour and energy of classic Amiga action platformers, but must not copy their characters, names, artwork, maps, music or story elements.
