## Recommended direction

The preferred story is **The Misfiled Storm**. The ancient tower already visible
in Sparkpaw's title and rear parallax is a weather archive. Its caretaker,
**Grand Archivolt**, has interpreted an old order to “file every storm” rather
too literally. He is pulling storms, Stormstone shards and loose machinery into
the tower, sorting the entire sky into numbered drawers. The Storm Ruins are
failing because weather is no longer allowed to happen in the proper places.

Sparkpaw discovers this when a homemade Stormstone detector—unfortunately also
a toast warmer—wakes him with a burnt slice and a cyan alarm. He follows the
shard trail toward the tower to return the stolen storm energy and convince,
disable or thoroughly re-file Archivolt.

This direction uses established project identity rather than introducing a
detached rescue plot:

- the distant cyan-cored tower remains the visible destination;
- the existing Stormstone, storm water, conduits, Striders and beetles become
  parts of one failing maintenance system;
- Sparkpaw's inventor identity motivates the quest;
- the tone can alternate between real stakes and dry mechanical absurdity.

The story hierarchy is now explicit:

- the **Stormstone** was the single ancient crystal regulating stored weather;
- Archivolt broke it into several large **Stormstone Cores**, one held at the
  end of each level;
- the current small diamond collectibles are **Storm Shards**, minor splinters
  that charge Sparkpaw's gauntlet; collecting fifty retains the current extra-
  life behavior;
- recovering every Core opens the route to Archivolt and allows Sparkpaw to
  reassemble the Stormstone.
