## Recommended direction

The preferred story is **The Misfiled Storm**. The ancient Stormstone weather
machine used five installed Cores to keep the valley in balance. Its caretaker,
**Grand Archivolt**, obeys a damaged order—`CONTAIN ALL WEATHER. RELEASE
NOTHING.`—by removing those Cores, sealing them in weather stations and
reversing the network. Each station now draws its element inward without end.

Sparkpaw sees the resulting storms tear through his home. His shard-powered
gauntlet can locate and safely carry the Cores, so he sets out to free one per
level and return all five to the Stormstone.

This direction uses established project identity rather than introducing a
detached rescue plot:

- the distant cyan-cored tower remains the visible destination;
- the existing Stormstone, storm water, conduits, Striders and beetles become
  parts of one failing maintenance system;
- Sparkpaw's inventor identity motivates the quest;
- the tone can alternate between real stakes and dry mechanical absurdity.

The story hierarchy is now explicit:

- the **Stormstone** is the central ancient weather machine;
- its five installed **Stormstone Cores** are Lightning, Rain, Wind, Warmth and
  Balance; Archivolt removed and sealed them away;
- the current small diamond collectibles are **Storm Shards**, minor splinters
  that charge Sparkpaw's gauntlet; collecting fifty retains the current extra-
  life behavior;
- recovering every Core lets Sparkpaw restore the Stormstone and natural sky.
