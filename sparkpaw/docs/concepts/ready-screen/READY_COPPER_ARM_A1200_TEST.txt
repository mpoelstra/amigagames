Sparkpaw ready-menu COP1LC arm candidate - real A1200 HD gate
=============================================================

This is the exact production-style executable and runtime asset set accepted
in the supplied FS-UAE/68030 HD test. Menu changes are completed in a hidden
display buffer. COP1LC is then armed safely without COPJMP1, allowing the
Copper to adopt the new list at its natural vertical-blank restart. There is
no diagnostic logger or profiling code.

Copy the complete Sparkpaw-CopperArm-A1200 drawer to the Amiga hard drive and
launch Sparkpaw-CopperArm from that drawer.

Test
----

1. Alternate START GAME and OPTIONS as rapidly as possible with Up/Down for
   at least two minutes.
2. Enter OPTIONS and alternate JUMP and FIRE as rapidly as possible with
   Left/Right for at least two minutes.
3. Watch for even a single mixed selector, partial rail, broken glyph, colour
   flash or disturbed corner graphic.
4. Re-enter OPTIONS several times and confirm the chosen value is retained.
5. Select START GAME and confirm the normal fade and gameplay transition.

Please report whether any intermittent glitch remains. Passing this ZIP proves
only the ordinary HD path on the tested real A1200; it does not establish ADF
or WHDLoad acceptance.

Result
------

Rejected on the supplied real A1200. Intermittent fragments remained and could
appear after 15 seconds or more while the selected screen was completely idle.
That result ruled out COP1LC/COPJMP1 publication as the sole cause and led to
the stale sprite-DMA ownership correction.
