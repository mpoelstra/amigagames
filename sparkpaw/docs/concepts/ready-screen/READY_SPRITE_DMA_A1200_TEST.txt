Sparkpaw ready-screen sprite-DMA candidate - real A1200 HD gate
================================================================

This ZIP contains the exact production-style executable and runtime assets
accepted in the supplied FS-UAE/68030 HD test. Hardware-sprite DMA remains off
while the title/loading/ready Copper lists are active and is enabled only when
the gameplay Copper list is installed. Hidden ready buffers and COP1LC-only
menu publication remain enabled. There is no logger or profiling code.

Copy the complete Sparkpaw-SpriteDMA-A1200 drawer to the Amiga hard drive and
launch Sparkpaw-SpriteDMA from that drawer.

Test
----

1. Leave START GAME selected without touching a control for at least three
   minutes. Watch for any isolated flash or fragment.
2. Switch once to OPTIONS and leave it untouched for at least three minutes.
3. Rapidly alternate START GAME/OPTIONS and JUMP/FIRE for at least two minutes.
4. Start gameplay and confirm Sparkpaw and the normal gameplay sprites remain
   visible and correct.

Please report even a single intermittent glitch. Passing this ZIP establishes
only the ordinary HD path on the tested real A1200, not ADF or WHDLoad.

Result
------

Accepted on the supplied real A1200/68030 HD path. No ready-screen glitches
were observed, and gameplay entry retained normal sprite presentation. This
does not establish ADF or WHDLoad runtime acceptance.
