Sparkpaw ready-screen sprite-DMA candidate - FS-UAE/68030 HD gate
=================================================================

Purpose
-------

This production-style candidate retains the hidden ready buffers and COP1LC-
only menu publication. Its one additional change keeps hardware-sprite DMA off
while title/loading/ready Copper lists are active, because those lists do not
initialize sprite pointers. Sprite DMA is enabled only when the gameplay
Copper list is installed. It contains no logger or profiling code.

Test
----

1. Launch Sparkpaw-SpriteDMA in the normal FS-UAE/68030 A1200 HD setup.
2. Leave START GAME selected without touching any control for at least two
   minutes. Watch the entire screen for any isolated flash or fragment.
3. Switch once to OPTIONS and leave it untouched for at least two minutes.
4. Rapidly alternate START GAME/OPTIONS and JUMP/FIRE for at least two minutes.
5. Start gameplay and confirm Sparkpaw and all normal gameplay sprites remain
   visible and correct.

Supplied FS-UAE/68030 HD testing accepts the idle START GAME and OPTIONS
screens, rapid menu switching and the transition into gameplay. This does not
establish real-A1200, ADF, WHDLoad or FS-UAE/68020 acceptance. The matching
real-A1200 HD ZIP is the next gate.
