SPARKPAW SCORE SYSTEM - FS-UAE/68030 REGRESSION ROUTE

Historical unnumbered visual gate, updated with the accepted alpha.68 prompt
and replay behaviour so it remains usable as a regression route.

Hypothesis
----------
The empty right HUD panel can show a four-digit event-driven score without
disturbing gameplay presentation, and touching the Stormstone Core can leave
the gameplay renderer cleanly for a tear-free 64-colour tally screen.

Setup
-----
- A1200 / AGA, PAL, 68030, 2 MB Chip RAM and 8 MB Fast RAM.
- Launch Sparkpaw-Score-Test from this drawer.
- This is a production/visual build: it intentionally writes no performance
  log. The accepted 68020 cadence gate follows only after this visual gate.

Controls
--------
- A/D or joystick: move
- W or configured second button: jump
- S/down: crouch
- Space or joystick Fire: plasma / menu confirmation

Test route
----------
1. Collect several diamonds and defeat at least one beetle or Strider.
2. Check that the score at far right is sharp, stable and increases by 5 per
   diamond and 20 only for the first defeat of an authored enemy spawn.
3. Lose one life if practical; score and level time should not reset.
   The time bonus uses a 120-second par and becomes zero at or beyond 120
   accumulated seconds across all attempts.
4. Reach and touch the Stormstone Core at the far right.
5. Check the native LEVEL COMPLETE screen: fixed columns, enemy/diamond/time
   counts transferring into TOTAL SCORE, one short tick per visible step, no
   tearing, stale gameplay graphics or audio corruption. After tallying,
   `REPLAY LEVEL` should appear centred at the bottom.
6. Fire may finish the active row immediately. After the final total, press
   Fire once: the score screen should fade fully to black and restart Level 1
   almost immediately with score, time, lives and encounters reset. No LOADING
   screen or top-edge diamond flash should appear. This resident Level-1 replay
   is temporary one-level flow; a later
   multi-level flow will replace it rather than reuse this shortcut.

Please report separately:
- startup and gameplay still correct;
- HUD score readable and stable;
- result transition and composition correct;
- tally alignment, cadence and sound pleasant;
- Fire skip/final Level 1 replay correct and free of a Workbench/level flash
  or one-frame lower-screen glitch;
- any flicker, tearing, silence, distorted tick or crash.
