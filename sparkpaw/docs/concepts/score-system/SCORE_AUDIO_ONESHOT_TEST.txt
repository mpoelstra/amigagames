Sparkpaw alpha.66 one-shot audio acceptance
============================================

Scope
-----

Phase 6C.8 changes only Paula effect lifecycle. Generated sample bytes,
volumes, trigger sites, cooldowns, priorities and channel ownership remain
unchanged. Player plasma stays on channel 0; prioritized gameplay effects stay
on channel 1; channels 2-3 remain reserved.

Confirmed source defect
-----------------------

Alpha.65's tally sample is 496 bytes, about 45 ms at Paula period 322. Its
hardcoded three-field DMA lifetime kept channel 1 active for 60 ms, so Paula
reloaded the sample beginning for roughly 15 ms. Other hardcoded lifetimes also
differed from their sample byte lengths and Storm Triumph was truncated.

Correction
----------

Each channel now disables DMA, waits for two deterministic raster-line changes,
starts the requested sample, waits for Paula to latch it and redirects the next
reload to a two-byte Chip-RAM silence word. Active duration is the ceiling of
the byte-derived PAL-field duration plus one guard field for call paths that
update audio in the starting field.

Supplied evidence
-----------------

FS-UAE A1200/68030 HD A/B captures retain identical 48-kHz OBS settings and
runtime assets. Baseline tally bursts remain above threshold for about 61 ms;
the candidate bursts last about 41 ms and contain no second onset. MrDig reports
that B sounds better, while remaining unsure whether the sample timbre itself
is fully perfect.

A supplied phone recording of the unnumbered candidate on the real
A1200/68030 from HD shows Core completion, the full unskipped tally, final
prompt, replay loading and return to gameplay. It contains no persistent whine,
new hard restart click or missing tally sequence, and no baseline-style second
onset after the source sample ends. Room/speaker response prevents treating the
phone waveform as direct-capture equivalence.

Acceptance boundary
-------------------

Accept the one-shot lifecycle and improvement in supplied FS-UAE/68030 HD and
focused real-A1200/68030 HD evidence. Do not claim that every sample's artistic
timbre is final. FS-UAE/68020, ADF, WHDLoad, Analogue Pocket and broader
real-hardware alpha.66 runtime acceptance remain pending.
