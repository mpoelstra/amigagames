SPARKPAW PROJECTILE SWEEP A/B - FS-UAE 68020

Candidate diagnostic only: unnumbered, not a release.

A Pixel is the accepted per-pixel collision reference. B Sweep is the
FS-UAE/68030-accepted coalesced first-hit candidate. Both use the centred camera,
HUD score fast-copy and identical detailed profiling.

Use stock A1200/68020 settings. Play each for 30-60 seconds over as similar a
busy route as practical. Fire heavily in both directions, collect diamonds and
fight beetles and Striders. Check that impacts, hits, deaths and sounds remain
correct. Do not touch the Core.

Press the left mouse button once during gameplay. The image freezes while
renderdiag.log is written. Wait several seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE. Leave
each log in its own drawer. Compare projectile average, p95 and maximum first;
the detailed profiler materially lowers displayed cadence, so its FPS is only
a like-for-like secondary signal.
