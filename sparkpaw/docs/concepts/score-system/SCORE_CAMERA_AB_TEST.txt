SPARKPAW SCORE/CAMERA 68020 CADENCE A/B

Candidate only: unnumbered, not a release.

Hypothesis
----------
The accepted centred camera may increase rolling-column frequency relative to
the old 105..202px dead zone. This A/B changes only that camera rule. Both
drawers otherwise contain the current score system, assets, gameplay, rolling
renderer and identical minimal-cadence instrumentation.

Builds
------
- A Centre: current preferred camera, Sparkpaw centred at screen x=160.
- B Deadzone: measurement reference using the former 105..202px dead zone.

Test route
----------
1. Use stock A1200/68020 FS-UAE settings for both drawers.
2. Run each for 30-60 seconds over as similar a route as practical.
3. Stress both equally: hold/repeatedly press Fire, collect diamonds, fight the
   same busy enemy area, and keep moving/jumping.
4. Do not touch the Core. During ordinary gameplay press the left mouse button
   once. The image should freeze while `renderdiag.log` is saved. Wait several
   seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE.
5. Leave each log in its original drawer.

Acceptance and interpretation
-----------------------------
This A/B diagnoses cost; it does not propose restoring the inferior deadzone.
Compare effective FPS, one/two/three-field intervals, rolling-column events,
ownership violations and perceived smoothness. A material B advantage means
the renderer should be optimized to retain A's centred composition.
