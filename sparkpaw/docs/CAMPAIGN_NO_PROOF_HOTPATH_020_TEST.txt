Sparkpaw no-proof-hotpath Level-1 cadence gate
==============================================

This stock-68020 minimal-cadence build is identical in gameplay and renderer
structure to the accepted direct-call candidate. It additionally excludes the
focused Stormrail screenshot-capture selector from campaign gameplay. Dedicated
Stormrail proof executables retain those checks.

Play a representative Level-1 route including a busy combat section. After the
run, press the left mouse button once. The image freezes while renderdiag.log
is written. Wait a few seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE.

Compare against
---------------

- alpha.68: 48.58 FPS, 2.9% two-field, zero three-plus;
- direct calls with proof selector: 48.42 FPS, 3.26% two-field, zero
  three-plus;
- bound indirect operations: 47.25 FPS, 5.8% two-field;
- integrated renderer: 44.56 FPS, 11.9% missed frames.

Require zero ownership violations, no functional or visual regression and a
matched or improved normalized distribution versus the direct-call result.
