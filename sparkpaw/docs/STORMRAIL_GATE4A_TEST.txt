SPARKPAW STORMRAIL GATE 4A - 68030 HD TEST
===========================================

This drawer is the first subjective Gate-4 debris-run test. It preserves the
accepted four Gate-3 formations and adds a short deterministic 17-object
ruin passage after formation four. Do not use it for the final 68020 verdict.

Run the drawer's Sparkpaw executable with the usual A1200/68030 FS-UAE setup.
Board the Skimmer and play through all four formations. After the fourth wave:

1. Arches, slabs, pillars and small ruin pieces should cross horizontally,
   vertically and diagonally at visibly different speeds. The climax reaches
   four obstacle Bobs; earlier passages should remain less dense.
2. Every shot impact should be audible. Destructible pieces blink briefly;
   massed solid pieces absorb the shot without blinking and must be dodged.
3. Small destructible masonry takes two hits. A large destructible piece takes
   six and shows the damaged state for its final three hit points.
4. Ordinary debris disappears on destruction and does not split into more
   hazards. One authored large loot block releases a canonical diamond.
5. Large destruction gives 20 points and small destruction gives 5 points.
6. Touching any large or small piece costs one half-heart, plays the accepted
   hurt sound and consumes that obstacle instead of repeatedly draining life.
7. The fourth free diamond line follows the obstacle encounter and retains the
   existing Level-1 pickup sound and five-point value.

Please judge these subjective points on 68030:

- Does the intact object read as carved castle-ruin masonry against every
  route palette rather than as a generic asteroid or sci-fi object?
- Is six hits enough to create pressure without feeling like a bullet sponge?
- Is the cracked frame obvious at native scale?
- Does the mixture create a fun choice between shooting and route planning?
- Is it immediately clear after one shot which pieces can be destroyed?
- Do audio, collisions, score, diamonds and all four older formations remain
  correct across a death/restart?
- Do you see any masks, trails, stale fragments, loading hang or slowdown?

Stop after this test and report what you saw. A second concurrent large rock,
falling debris, clouds, 1UP, finale and full route are deliberately absent.
