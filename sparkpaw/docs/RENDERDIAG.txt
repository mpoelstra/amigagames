SPARKPAW PRODUCTION RENDER DIAGNOSTIC
=====================================

rd02 measures the optimized production 4+3 renderer. The ordinary HD
`sparkpaw` now contains the same high-water logger and clean left-mouse exit,
so a separate diagnostic drawer is no longer needed. It does not display or
test REAR16. Active diamonds now persist in the clean/display world and retain
their eight-position hover while movement updates are staggered over four
frames. Start `Sparkpaw-Renderdiag` from this complete drawer, play through
the level normally and deliberately exercise busy real situations:

- fire rapidly while enemies and diamond trails are visible;
- follow both Striders through their routes;
- reach the water region and trigger the splash;
- backtrack so parked/respawning encounters are exercised.

Click the left mouse button when finished. The HD build restores Workbench and
writes `renderdiag.log` beside the executable. Return that log and, if anything
looked abnormal, a recording. The log must identify:

  2026-08-14-rd02-staggered-diamond-high-water

It records only the single slowest real production frame: raster duration,
camera position, wraps and exact restore/draw counts for projectiles, beetles,
Striders, diamonds, splash and water. No DOS writes occur during takeover.

The bootable ADF remains reset-to-exit and performs no DOS log write.
