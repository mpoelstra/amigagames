Sparkpaw physical split Level-1 cadence gate
============================================

This is the stock-68020 minimal-cadence gate for the functionally accepted
split-renderer campaign candidate. Level 1 and Stormrail are separate compiled
renderer units selected by one section dispatcher. This build starts directly
in Level 1 and holds the completed frame for evidence capture.

Play a normal representative Level-1 route, including a busy combat section.
After the run, press the left mouse button once. The image freezes while the
bounded renderdiag.log is written. Wait a few seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE.

Compare against
---------------

- protected alpha.68: 48.58 FPS, 33 two-field, zero three-field out of 1,137;
- renderer-isolation proof: 48.23 FPS, 101 two-field, zero three-plus out of
  the longer 2,755 run;
- rejected integrated renderer: 44.56 FPS, 217 two-field and three three-plus
  out of 1,845.

Require zero ownership violations, no visual/gameplay regression and cadence
materially matching the 48 FPS isolated/alpha.68 range.
