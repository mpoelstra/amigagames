Sparkpaw integrated Level-1 renderer A/B baseline
=================================================

This is the unchanged integrated-campaign stock-68020 baseline with minimal
cadence logging. It is A in a causal A/B against the renderer-isolation drawer.

Play a representative Level-1 route, including a busy combat section, as
similarly as practical to B. After the run, press the left mouse button once.
The image freezes while renderdiag.log is written. Wait a few seconds and then
stop/reset FS-UAE.
