Sparkpaw Stage 2 dynamic synchronization B: direct word-copy prototype

Run this self-contained drawer in the identical FS-UAE/68030 configuration and
with approximately the same workload as A. This candidate changes only how
canonical water/collectible words are copied to the inactive rolling target.
Pixels, timing eligibility, animation state and target ownership are unchanged.

Play at least 30 seconds. Move, sprint, jump, fire and reach at least the first
Strider. Exit with the left mouse button and leave renderdiag.log here.

Check carefully for diamond/water corruption, trails, flicker, the ground/HUD
seam and general Bob corruption. This is a diagnostic prototype, not production
or 68020/ADF/Pocket/real-hardware acceptance. A clean 68030 result is required
before a separate 68020 measurement drawer may be tested.
