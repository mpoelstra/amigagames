SPARKPAW STORMRAIL PACING 3 - FINITE DEBRIS FIELD
=================================================

This replaces the rejected Pacing-2 tail. Run Sparkpaw-Pacing3 with the usual
A1200/68030 FS-UAE setup.

The complete 48-event debris field is authored as one passage. Large ruin
blocks, pillar fragments and shards are interleaved across the whole duration;
their heights, diagonal drift, speed and left/right entry are distributed over
the complete table rather than appended as a second repeated section.

The field now has a hard endpoint. At monotone distance 11200 the presentation
camera stops and all Flight-owned enemies, shots, rewards and debris are
cleared. Nothing may spawn or scroll after that point. This is the temporary
development-slice ending, not a results screen or campaign transition.

The fourth free diamond line was moved before debris. Inside the debris field,
diamonds may come only from the two destructible large loot carriers. Formation
rewards remain four compact diamonds when a complete formation is destroyed.
