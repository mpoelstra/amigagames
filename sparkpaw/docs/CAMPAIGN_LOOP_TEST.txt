===========================================

Status: candidate; hosttests and 68020 compile pass, native feel pending.

This candidate writes only one bounded `back-title-trace.log` when BACK TO
TITLE is selected. It performs no per-frame logging. If the transition stalls,
leave FS-UAE stopped on that frame so Codex can inspect the completed phases.

Mount this drawer in FS-UAE and run Sparkpaw-Campaign. Codex has not booted the
staged executable; the visible/native checks below are yours.

Check the complete path:

At any safe gameplay moment in both Storm Ruins and Stormrail, press Esc once.
The active run must fade/blank cleanly, show no Workbench or stale gameplay,
skip the story intro, rebuild the normal presentation and stop at the ready
screen. `START AT` must be reset to `STORM RUINS`; nothing from the abandoned
score, vitals, diamonds, enemies, projectiles or awards may carry forward.

1. Let the complete five-plate story intro run (or test its existing skip),
   then confirm the large title and ready/menu screen wait for your own
   `START GAME` Fire/Space input. In OPTIONS, confirm the visible `SECOND
   BUTTON` and `START AT` rows. Up/Down selects a row, Left/Right changes it,
   and Fire returns to the ready screen without starting. `START AT` defaults
   to `STORM RUINS`. Selecting `STORMRAIL`, returning, then confirming `START
   GAME` must load departure/boarding with 3 lives, full health, 0 diamonds and
   score 0000. Confirm the original
   Level-1 world, detailed rear background (no replacement green band), floor,
   collision, player start, enemies, diamonds, shots and HUD are unchanged.
   At the final ruin, confirm the large Stormstone Core is visible before it
   is collected and that its collection still advances normally.
2. Finish Level 1. Let the complete tally run once. Confirm two choices:
   REPLAY LEVEL and CONTINUE. Both labels must remain horizontally centred in
   the alpha.68 cyan/blue style while the fixed yellow score-colour arrow
   changes rows. CONTINUE must always be selected when the menu first becomes
   interactive, including when the Stormstone was collected while Jump/W was
   held. Release all directions once; every subsequent distinct Up or Down
   press must toggle exactly once, while holding it must not repeat. Test Fire.
3. First test REPLAY LEVEL. It must fade to black and restart Level 1 without
   a loading screen. No score, diamonds, enemies, timer or input may carry.
4. Finish again and choose CONTINUE. It must fade fully black, show the normal
   loading presentation without a palette/DMA glitch, and begin Stormrail at
   departure/boarding. The exact remaining lives, half-heart health and current
   diamond meter from Level 1 must carry into Stormrail, while the Stormrail
   score starts at 0000. No Workbench or stray disk icon may appear.
5. Complete the full Stormrail route and Harrier finale. Confirm its accepted
   results tally and two choices: REPLAY LEVEL and BACK TO TITLE. BACK TO TITLE
   must always be selected when the menu first becomes interactive. As above,
   stale completion input must not move it and each fresh Up/Down press must
   toggle exactly once.
6. Test REPLAY LEVEL. It must restart resident at Stormrail departure with no
   loading screen and no local score/time/pickup/award/finale carry-over. It
   must restore the same Level-1 lives, health and diamond meter snapshot, and
   its section score must again start at 0000. The approach rear mountains and
   sky must match the first entry exactly: no retained green/teal horizontal
   route-palette bands or distorted colour regions.
7. Complete Stormrail again and choose BACK TO TITLE. Confirm it does not stay
   black: the large title appears, followed by the existing ready/start screen,
   then a fresh Level 1.
8. Listen for tally audio, look for black/loading transition glitches, and
   report menu timing, selection clarity and overall campaign-loop feel.
