Sparkpaw Stage 5G: guarded AGA32 gameplay candidate
====================================================

This HD diagnostic build integrates the Stage 5F-accepted left-prefetch
geometry into the Stage 5E gameplay renderer. The scrolling playfields use
`$20..$d0`, a 48-byte fetch and four valid physical guard bytes. FRONT reads
the guard inside its existing middle ring copy. REAR uses a dedicated guarded
display copy; no pointer reads before an allocation. The fixed HUD remains on
its previously proven `$30..$d0`/44-byte fetch.

The accepted `camera + 16` phase, world coordinates, collision, parallax,
assets, palettes, sprites, Bobs, animations and gameplay rules are unchanged.

First gate: FS-UAE/68030 HD
---------------------------

Traverse in both directions and repeatedly cross 16- and 32-pixel camera
boundaries. Exercise sprint, jump, shooting, collectibles, beetles and the
first Striders. Check particularly:

- no intermittent black or displaced strip at the left gameplay edge;
- no teleport, HUD inset or gameplay/HUD boundary regression;
- no trails, corrupt Bobs or collision mismatch;
- normal rear parallax and stable hardware sprites.

Click left mouse after the run to flush `renderdiag.log`. Supply a movie only
if a temporal defect remains; otherwise the log plus a short result report is
sufficient. Do not test FS-UAE/68020 or real hardware until this presentation
gate is reviewed. This is not an ADF or release candidate.
