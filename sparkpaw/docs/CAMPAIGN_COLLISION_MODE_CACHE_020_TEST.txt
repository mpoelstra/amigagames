Sparkpaw collision-mode-cache Level-1 cadence gate
=================================================

This stock-68020 minimal-cadence build uses the 68030-accepted section-local
collision-mode cache. Level-1 collision probes retain their existing geometry
but no longer call gameStormrailActive() for every tile or pixel query.

Play a representative Level-1 route including busy combat, jumping and shots.
After the run, press the left mouse button once. The image freezes while
renderdiag.log is written. Wait a few seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE.

Compare against
---------------

- alpha.68: 48.58 FPS, 2.9% two-field, zero three-plus;
- direct-call best: 48.42 FPS, 3.26% two-field, zero three-plus;
- no-proof neutral run: 48.32 FPS, 3.46% two-field, zero three-plus;
- bound indirect operations: 47.25 FPS, 5.8% two-field.

Require zero ownership violations, no visual/gameplay regression and a
materially improved normalized distribution rather than average-only movement.
