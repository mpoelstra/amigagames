Sparkpaw Stage 5D5: aligned FMODE1 phase correction
===================================================

This is the final isolated origin-correction proof, not the game. Stage 5D4
measured the FMODE1 bitplane marker exactly one 16-logical-pixel calibration
cell to the right of the matched FMODE0 reference. D5 keeps `$30..$d0`, 44-byte
fetches and longword-aligned pointers, but biases the logical FMODE1 fetch phase
by +16 pixels. The 32-pixel coarse pointer changes only where the corresponding
extended fine-delay phase wraps. No misaligned pointer or DDF shift is used.

The initial phase is held for 150 PAL fields. Take one screenshot during those
first three seconds, then let the proof sweep for at least six seconds. The
cyan/white bitplane marker should return to the FMODE0 reference position from
Stage 5D4. The fixed hardware-sprite marker must remain independent of the
sweep. Look for any jump halfway through or at the 32-pixel boundary, changes
in visible width, left-border pixels, or movement in the lower reference.

Click left mouse to exit and preserve `renderbench.log`. A movie is needed only
if a temporal jump or corruption is visible. Test only in FS-UAE/68030 HD at
this gate; it provides no gameplay-framerate, 68020, ADF, Analogue Pocket or
real-hardware acceptance.
