Stormrail Gate 2 compact-flight real-A1200 HD test
=================================================

This is an unnumbered direct-start visual/control candidate, not alpha.69.
It does not contain an ADF, WHDLoad package, enemies, hazards or campaign flow.

Target
------

PAL A1200/AGA with 2 MB Chip and 8 MB Fast RAM. The supplied accelerated
68030 machine is the first hardware gate.

Run
---

Copy the complete Stormrail-G2-A1200-HD drawer to the Amiga hard drive and run
Stormrail-Test from inside it. Keep the assets drawer beside the executable.

Controls
--------

- Arrow keys or joystick: board and fly in four directions
- Fire / Ctrl: rapid fire in the shooter section
- Esc: quit when supported by the launch environment

Inspect
-------

1. The accepted boarding and full-size departure remain intact.
2. Fade-out/fade-in changes to the compact 80x32 shooter vehicle cleanly.
3. No black pixels appear behind/right of Paw.
4. No coloured fragments, duplicate vehicle pieces or disappearing underside.
5. Palette transitions, rear parallax, HUD, cyan motor and shots remain stable.
6. Movement reaches all four fair screen limits and still feels responsive.

Please also warm-reset once if practical, start Stormrail-Test again and check
the compact vehicle a second time. The candidate explicitly clears its native
Chip-RAM caches during every initialization.
