SPARKPAW STORMRAIL DEBRIS 1.1 - 68030 ART TEST
================================================

This is an art-only comparison against the accepted Debris 1.0 gameplay
reference. All 28 events, six object slots, paths, speeds, HP, hit flashes,
collisions, score, diamond drops and animation timing are unchanged.

Run the drawer's Sparkpaw executable with the usual A1200/68030 FS-UAE setup
and play through the debris field after formation four.

Please judge only the revised native masonry family:

1. Do the arch, slab, pillar and fragments now read as irregular weathered
   castle-ruin debris rather than clean geometric blocks?
2. Are the chipped silhouettes, uneven courses, broken faces and restrained
   stone highlights richer without becoming noisy against any route palette?
3. Does the stronger difference between the two tumble poses improve motion,
   or does it look like distracting shape wobble?
4. Is the large cracked loot block still immediately readable while firing and
   steering through the dense field?
5. Report any mask trail, clipped edge, loading hang or slowdown.

Debris 1.0 remains archived as the exact rollback baseline. This test contains
no gameplay changes, 1UP, clouds, route extension, finale or 68020 verdict.
