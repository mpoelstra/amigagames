Sparkpaw campaign split-renderer functional gate
================================================

This unnumbered 68030 functional candidate introduces the first physical
renderer boundary: Level 1 and Stormrail are compiled as separate renderer
objects and a small dispatcher owns exactly the currently loaded section.
Gameplay, art, input, scores, transitions and asset files are unchanged.

Test the ordinary complete flow from the large title and ready menu. Check:

- Level 1 visuals, enemies, diamonds, Core and results;
- Continue loading into Stormrail;
- departure/boarding, flight, finale and results;
- Stormrail replay starts with an intact departure background;
- Back to Title returns through the large title to the ready screen;
- Escape from gameplay returns to ready;
- Level-1 replay remains intact.

This build has no performance logger. Report presentation, corruption,
transitions, replay/reset behaviour and feel. Stock-68020 cadence follows only
after this functional gate is accepted.
