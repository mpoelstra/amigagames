SPARKPAW - STORMRAIL INTERLUDE V2

This is an isolated, unnumbered Phase 6D gameplay slice. It skips the intro
and Level 1, does not alter campaign progression and does not change the ADF.

RUN
  Use FS-UAE with an A1200 / 68030 configuration.
  Mount dist and open: Stormrail-V2-FSUAE-030-HD
  Start: Stormrail-Test

CONTROLS
  Approach: left/right moves Sparkpaw. Walk to the parked Skimmer and jump
  so Sparkpaw descends into the open cockpit. There is deliberately no
  proximity/Fire teleport and no automatic boarding timer in this build.
  Flight: joystick moves in four directions. Hold FIRE for rapid shots.
  Press the left mouse button when finished to save stormrail-proof.log and
  exit this test executable.

PLEASE JUDGE THIS FOCUSED CORRECTION
  1. Can Sparkpaw walk all the way to the Skimmer on visible solid ground,
     without an invisible wall or walking in the air?
  2. Does jumping and descending into the cockpit trigger boarding clearly?
  3. Is the richer v3 parallax background a substantial visual improvement
     over V1/V2, without three obvious empty horizontal strips?
  Stop judging after the boarding transition if useful. The corridor remains
  rejected and is deliberately outside this focused correction check.

CURRENT SCOPE
  This is an early coherent V2 slice, intentionally offered before polishing
  the full 2.5-minute encounter. It includes the corrected approach, physical
  jump-to-board trigger and richer v3 background. Existing flight mechanics
  remain present only so boarding has a reachable next state. Route art is
  rejected and not representative: authored corridor design,
  encounter scripting, a complete failure/restart flow, final audio, results
  and campaign integration remain after the direction and feel are accepted.
