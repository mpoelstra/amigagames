SPARKPAW STORMRAIL SUNLIT DUST V5 - FS-UAE 68020 CADENCE

This is the 68020 cadence gate for the 68030-accepted Gate 4D dust field.
Gameplay, debris, enemy patterns and the eight independent dust/grit slots are
identical to the accepted visual build. Only low-overhead presentation cadence,
ownership and player-shot counters are enabled.

Use the stock A1200/68020 FS-UAE configuration and play the complete focused
Stormrail route normally. Fire regularly, especially during the debris field
and five-enemy formations.

At the final empty area, press the LEFT MOUSE BUTTON once. The image will
freeze; this is expected while renderdiag.log is saved. Wait a few seconds,
then stop or reset FS-UAE. Workbench will not return.

Please report whether motion felt smooth. Keep renderdiag.log in this drawer.
