Sparkpaw Stage 2 whole-codebase profiler (H7 presentation baseline)

Purpose
-------
This diagnostic build measures CPU sections with the CIA E-clock and records
median, p95, average, maximum and call counts. It also records logical audio
requests separately from actual Paula channel starts. It contains the accepted
H7 seam fix. It is not an optimization candidate or production build.

Current use
-----------
This drawer is ready for profiler validation in FS-UAE/68030. Run normally,
move through gameplay for at least 30 seconds, then exit with the left mouse
button. Wait for the frozen diagnostic screen. The log remains here as
renderdiag.log; no rename or move is needed.

Check only that presentation remains intact and that renderdiag.log contains
`median_ticks`, `p95_ticks` and `audio event=` lines. Matched performance-scene
drawers will be supplied separately after this instrumentation gate.

No production, 68020, ADF, Pocket or real-hardware acceptance may be inferred
from this diagnostic validation.
