Sparkpaw - Level 1 music integration (unnumbered HD candidate)

Launch Sparkpaw-Music from this drawer. First test: PAL A1200 / 68030,
2 MB Chip + 8 MB Fast. This is the normal complete game, not an A/B script.

Intro and title/READY retain their existing four-channel music. Starting
Storm Ruins plays Copper Sprint with all existing gameplay sound effects.
Water/life restarts keep music flowing. Results stop gameplay music and retain
the original tally effects. REPLAY LEVEL starts the track afresh without a
reload. CONTINUE goes to the unchanged SFX-only Stormrail. Escape returns to
READY/title music; OPTIONS -> Stormrail must not start Copper Sprint.

Play normally. Check the first music handoff, Escape and restarting Level 1.
When reaching results, check replay and then continuation to Stormrail.
Report any missing/stuck sound, click or broken transition and where it occurs.
No diagnostic log, automatic input or LMB-save/freeze is enabled. There is no
need to replay an identical timed route. Stop/reset the emulator normally.

Existing 0.7.0-alpha.4 release archives/ADFs are unchanged. This candidate is
HD only; ADF and WHDLoad adoption/packaging remain separate work.
