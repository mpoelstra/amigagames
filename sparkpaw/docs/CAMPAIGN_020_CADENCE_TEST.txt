SPARKPAW CURRENT CAMPAIGN - STOCK 68020 CADENCE

This is the low-overhead 68020 performance gate for the current campaign
candidate. Gameplay, renderer, assets and the complete Stormrail route are the
same as in the accepted Campaign-Loop1 build. Only aggregate cadence and
ownership counters are enabled. Campaign transition tracing is disabled so it
cannot contaminate the measurement.

Use the stock A1200/68020 FS-UAE configuration and start Sparkpaw-Camp-Cad.

1. Let the normal title and ready flow appear. Open OPTIONS, set START AT to
   STORMRAIL, press Fire to return, then select START GAME yourself.
2. Play the complete interlude normally. Move and fire throughout the busy
   route and the Harrier fight; do not wait deliberately in safe areas.
3. Confirm that boarding, backgrounds, debris, dust, HUD, 120-HP Harrier,
   attacks, contact damage, wall opening and automatic gate passage still look
   and behave like the accepted campaign candidate.
4. After the Skimmer has passed through the open gate, the final gameplay image
   intentionally remains visible instead of entering results. Press the LEFT
   MOUSE BUTTON once.
5. The image freezes while renderdiag.log is written. Wait three seconds, then
   stop/reset FS-UAE. Do not expect a return to Workbench.
6. Leave renderdiag.log in this drawer and tell Codex whether any route,
   transition, boss or gate section looked uneven.

The cadence sample begins at the established flight tick 400 and ends at
Gate-6 COMPLETE. Menus, loading, boarding preparation, results and post-finish
dwell are excluded. Acceptance requires zero ownership violations and no
meaningful regression from the accepted complete-interlude result of 49.97 FPS
(5175 one-field, one two-field and one three-field interval over 5177 samples).
The older general lower bound remains 48.58 FPS.

This is not a release, SemVer, ADF/multidisk or campaign-banking build.
