SPARKPAW STORMRAIL HEART 1 - 68030 VISUAL / FEEL TEST
=====================================================

This candidate keeps Debris5.2 intact and adds one native 16x21 AGA health
pickup. It is a classic red heart with a clean mask, authored directly at its
runtime pixel size and rendered through the existing bounded reward Bob pool.

- One fixed heart enters between the early formations, surrounded by three
  diamonds in a spacious triangular ring. The four pickups exactly fill the
  existing bounded reward pool and can be tested without reaching the debris
  field.
- The deliberately slower six-hit large debris carrier now drops a heart
  instead of a diamond. The earlier loot carrier still drops a diamond.
- A heart restores two half-heart HUD units: 1 -> 2 hearts, 1.5 -> 2.5,
  2.5 -> 3, and 3 remains 3.
- Pickup uses a stronger variant of the existing diamond sparkle sound.
- Heart and ring-diamond IDs are persistent across life restart, exactly like
  all other diamonds, so this group cannot be farmed by dying.

Please first take damage and collect the fixed heart, then if practical break
the slower large debris carrier. Judge the native art, pickup readability,
sound distinction and exact HUD restoration. Press LMB only when you want to
freeze and save renderdiag.log; wait a few seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE.
