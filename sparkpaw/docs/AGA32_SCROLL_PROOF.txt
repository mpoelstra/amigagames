Sparkpaw Stage 5D3: coordinated AGA 32-bit DDF calibration
========================================================

This is not the game and not a release candidate. It displays a synthetic
16-pixel grid while sweeping camera phases 0..63, four PAL fields per phase.
The 32-pixel boundary is deliberately crossed repeatedly. A fixed hardware
sprite bar at screen x=32 is compared with a unique bitplane marker authored at
source x=32. At phase zero their left edges must coincide. The lower 48 lines
use a separate fixed bitmap, matching the production HUD split structurally.

Purpose
-------

Test the measured Stage 5D2 fetch-half origin correction. The complete DDF
window moves from $30..$d0 to $38..$d8 while retaining the same fetch count,
44-byte modulos, longword pointers and accepted 0..31 phase sequence. Do not
infer gameplay, ADF or real-hardware acceptance from this executable.

Test first in FS-UAE/68030 HD. Record a short capture containing at least two
complete sweeps (about 11 seconds). Look specifically for:

- whether the hardware-sprite and bitplane marker coincide at phase zero;
- a jump at the 31-to-32 transition;
- changing width or colour of the far-left stripe;
- any movement in the lower fixed reference area;
- intermittent border pixels at the left edge.

Click the left mouse button to exit cleanly. The program writes
renderbench.log beside the executable. Do not spend a 68020 or real-hardware
run on this calibration until its phase/origin result has been reviewed.
