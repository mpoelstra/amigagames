SPARKPAW - STORMRAIL INTERLUDE V1

This is an isolated, unnumbered Phase 6D gameplay proof. It does not replace
Level 1, alter campaign progression or change the current ADF.

RUN
  Use FS-UAE with an A1200 / 68030 configuration.
  Mount this drawer as DH0 and start: Stormrail-Test
  The accepted disk/loading presentation appears briefly; intro and Level 1
  are skipped and the short Stormrail foot approach loads directly.

CONTROLS
  Short departure: left/right moves Sparkpaw; FIRE near the Skimmer boards it.
  The proof boards automatically after about seven seconds if FIRE is not used.
  Shooter: joystick moves freely; FIRE launches one cyan shot at a time.
  Press the left mouse button when finished. The test exits and writes
  stormrail-proof.log into this drawer.

WHAT TO CHECK
  1. The short foot section is immediately recognizable as departure setup.
  2. Boarding switches cleanly into fast forced horizontal parallax scrolling.
  3. The Skimmer remains readable while moving in all four directions.
  4. Flying enemies alternate between dart, square blocker and crescent/claw
     silhouettes. No Level-1 beetle is used.
  5. Controls and enemy pressure feel like a useful base for the full 2.5
     minute Bubble-and-Squeak-inspired interlude.

TEST SCOPE
  Play the available roughly 20-second scrolling slice several times if
  useful. Judge launch readability, boarding, steering, firing, collision,
  damage feedback, enemy silhouettes, route readability, background quality
  and overall feel. This is not yet the complete 2.5-minute encounter.

LIMITS OF V1
  This is the first mechanical/native-display slice, not a dist candidate.
  The unique Stormrail rear panorama, foreground corridor, Skimmer and three
  enemy silhouettes have passed an initial whole-display FS-UAE inspection.
  The generated dedicated Stormrail loading composition is visually rejected;
  the interlude will reuse the accepted disk/loading presentation. The first
  collision route now uses
  an inset Skimmer body, solid screen bounds, authored upper/lower obstruction
  volumes, health damage, 45-field invulnerability and blink feedback. A
  bounded unattended FS-UAE pass records three impacts and health 6 -> 3 while
  whole-display review shows the vehicle redirected before the turbine rims.
  Boarding animation, HUD/results, the complete 2.5-minute route, failure flow,
  final audio and the complete encounter script are not claimed complete yet.
