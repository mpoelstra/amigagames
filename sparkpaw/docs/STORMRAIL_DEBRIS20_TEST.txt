SPARKPAW STORMRAIL DEBRIS 2.0 - 68030 ART TEST
================================================

This is an art-only successor to the accepted Debris 1.0 gameplay baseline.
All 28 distance events, six object slots, paths, speeds, HP, hit flashes,
collisions, score, diamond drops and animation cadence remain unchanged.

Run the drawer's Sparkpaw-Debris2 executable with the usual A1200/68030
FS-UAE setup and play through the debris field after formation four.

Please judge the new native Storm Ruins masonry family:

1. Do the large, medium and small pieces now retain convincing volume,
   perspective and stone material at native gameplay scale?
2. Do the controlled tumble pairs read as the same mass changing orientation,
   without scale wobble, clipping or a rotating-cardboard appearance?
3. Is the destructible large block learnable through impact sound/flash, and
   is its damaged pose visible enough under pressure before it drops a diamond?
4. Does the busy field remain challenging and fun rather than visually muddy?
5. Report any mask trail, loading hang, stale object or slowdown.

This is the first subjective 68030 art/feel gate. No 68020 performance claim,
1UP, clouds, route extension, finale, Level 2 or campaign integration is
present. Debris 1.0 remains the exact rollback baseline in older-builds.
