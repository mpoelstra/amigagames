Sparkpaw direct renderer-call Level-1 cadence gate
=================================================

This stock-68020 minimal-cadence build uses the 68030-accepted physical
renderer split. The three per-frame operations branch on the active section at
their call site and then call the selected renderer directly; the prior bound
indirect function pointers are absent.

Play a normal representative Level-1 route, including a busy combat section.
After the run, press the left mouse button once. The image freezes while the
bounded renderdiag.log is written. Wait a few seconds, then stop/reset FS-UAE.

Compare against
---------------

- alpha.68: 48.58 FPS, 2.9% two-field, zero three-plus;
- isolated renderer proof: 48.23 FPS, 3.7% two-field, zero three-plus;
- bound indirect operations: 47.25 FPS, 5.8% two-field, zero three-plus;
- first physical split: 46.85 FPS, 6.7% two-field, zero three-plus;
- integrated renderer: 44.56 FPS, 11.9% missed frames.

Require zero ownership violations, no visual/gameplay regression and a clear
improvement over the bound-indirect result. Do not judge by average FPS alone;
compare the normalized two-field share and the user's observed busy-scene feel.
