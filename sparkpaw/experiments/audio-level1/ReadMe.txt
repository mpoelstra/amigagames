COPPER SPRINT - LEVEL 1 AUDIO PROOF

Open Audio-Level1 from Amiga Shell (or Workbench Execute Command).
First gate: FS-UAE PAL A1200 / 68030 / 2 MB Chip + 8 MB Fast.
Run from this complete drawer; it contains every required asset.

0-47 sec: Copper Sprint alone, 164 BPM, three music channels.
47-79 sec: all 15 existing sample files, one at a time, plus louder
health pickup. The console identifies each event.
79-88 sec: repeated plasma with hurt/death; low-priority jump requests
are deliberately suppressed. Then full Core and tally cues.
Automatic end at about 94 sec, or press LMB to stop early.
After audio stops, renderdiag.log is saved beside Audio-Level1.
This standalone proof returns to Shell/Workbench; it does not freeze.

Hear the groove, balance, jump, pickup, 1UP, water, enemy hit/death,
Strider, Harrier and complete Core/tally. Debris hit/break shares the
existing enemy hit/death samples. All source samples are unchanged.
This is an audio audition, not an actual gameplay route or new level.

Three hardware music voices; two software SFX voices mixed on Paula 3.
SFX therefore come from the left stereo side. Each has fixed half-gain
headroom plus its original event volume. The soundtrack is at 48/64.
A short foreground stall every three seconds in the second half should
NOT slow music. CIA music and audio-buffer IRQs remain enabled.

Report missing sounds, crackles, wrong speed, effect balance and whether
music stays steady. Keep renderdiag.log in this drawer for inspection.
The log records requests/starts/suppression, music ticks, mixed overlap
and coarse mixer raster cost. It is NOT complete music+SFX CPU timing.
Only after this first gate: separate 020 and hardware acceptance.

ptplayer 6.4 direct MOD replay; generated proof-only empty-channel
isolation. Small two-voice C SFX mixer, no general-purpose resampler.
No production game code, renderer or accepted intro/title is changed.
No native/emulator acceptance has yet been established.
